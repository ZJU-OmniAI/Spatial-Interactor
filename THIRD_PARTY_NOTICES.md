# Third-party software

This repository includes reduced source snapshots of the following projects:

- [LLaMA-Factory](https://github.com/hiyouga/LLaMA-Factory), Apache-2.0.
- [EasyR1](https://github.com/hiyouga/EasyR1), Apache-2.0.
- [verl](https://github.com/volcengine/verl), Apache-2.0, as included by EasyR1.

Their original copyright and license notices are retained in the corresponding
subdirectories. Model checkpoints and source media remain subject to their own
upstream licenses and terms.
